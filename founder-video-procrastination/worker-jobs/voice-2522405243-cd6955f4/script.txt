If procrastination were really about will power, why do we avoid the tasks that matter most? Psychologically, procrastination is often emotional avoidance. A task can trigger fear of failure, perfectionism, uncertainty, or overwhelm. Avoiding it gives your brain immediate relief, so the habit gets stronger. Instead of asking, "Why am I so lazy?" ask, "What feeling am I avoiding?" Then make the next step smaller: open the document, write one line, or work for just two minutes. Progress begins with feeling safe, not judging yourself.
