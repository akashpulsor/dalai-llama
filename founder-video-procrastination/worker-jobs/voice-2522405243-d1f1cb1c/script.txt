Procrastination is not simply a will power problem. Psychologically, it is often emotional avoidance. Important tasks can trigger fear of failure, perfectionism, uncertainty, or overwhelm. Avoidance gives quick relief, so the habit gets stronger. Instead of asking, "Why am I lazy?" ask, "What feeling am I avoiding?" Then make the next step smaller: open the document, write one line, or work for two minutes. Progress starts with feeling safe, not judging yourself.
