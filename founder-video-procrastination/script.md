# Founder Video Script: Procrastination Is Not About Willpower

## Target

- Duration: 30 seconds
- Format: 9:16 founder-led portrait video
- Tone: warm, grounded, non-judgmental, psychologically informed
- Voice: cloned from the consented founder reference video

## Exact Spoken Script

If procrastination were really about will power, why do we avoid the tasks that matter most? Psychologically, procrastination is often emotional avoidance. A task can trigger fear of failure, perfectionism, uncertainty, or overwhelm. Avoiding it gives your brain immediate relief, so the habit gets stronger. Instead of asking, "Why am I so lazy?" ask, "What feeling am I avoiding?" Then make the next step smaller: open the document, write one line, or work for just two minutes.

## Content Notes

- The opening question is the hook.
- The mechanism is framed as "often" rather than as a universal diagnosis.
- The practical step is intentionally small and non-clinical.
- This is educational mental-health content, not medical advice.

## Evidence Basis

- Procrastination can function as short-term mood regulation through avoidance of difficult task-related emotions.
- Stress, task aversiveness, emotion-regulation difficulty, and perfectionism can increase vulnerability to procrastination.
